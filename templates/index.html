How the AI works

EcoWise uses synthetic training data with plastic usage, delivery frequency, shopping frequency, recycling habits, and reusable item usage. A Random Forest model learns behavior patterns and returns category probabilities.

  • Higher plastic, delivery, and shopping increase risk.
  • Recycling and reusable items reduce emissions.
  • Probability meters explain uncertainty clearly.

Carbon Footprint Formula

Carbon Footprint =
(Plastic Usage x 2.5)
+ (Food Delivery x 1.8)
+ (Shopping x 1.5)
- (Recycling x 2.0)
- (Reusable Items x 1.7)

Plastic and packaging increase pollution, while recycling and reusable products reduce landfill waste and carbon footprint.
